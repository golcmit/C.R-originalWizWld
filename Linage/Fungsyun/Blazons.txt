
Escutcheon:  Party per chevron; Chief Sable the Southern Cross Argent ,the fifth star extending Argent lines to the others, Base Azure a Chinese junk Or.

Crest: On a wreath Sable and Or, a pheasant Gules pecking at the shield.

Supporters: Dexter, a Shouxing (Old Man of the South Pole) Proper holding in his sinister hand a staff Tenné; Sinister, a Guan Yu holding Properin his dexter hand a Qinglong Yanyue Dao Azure.

Motto: On an escroll beneath the shield, "紐帶招引珍寶".
